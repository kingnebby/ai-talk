What is
a phone?

  • 3 core pieces: cpu, mem, data link

The
first one.

  • The first mobile phone ever invented
  • Prototype demonstrated: April 3, 1973
  • Released: 1984 (10 years)
  • Stored 30 Phone Numbers
  • No internet. No apps. No calculator.

The first
smart one.

  • The first "smart phone"
  • Prototype demonstrated: 1992
  • Released: August 16, 1994
  • CPU: 16 MHz
  • email, fax, calendar, touchscreen and software applications
  • The term "smart phone" didn't arrive until 2000

6,700×.

  • You can't compare the DynaTAC, so we have to compare Simon to the modern iPhone.
  • 16 MHz -> 4,930 MHz
  • 1 CPU -> 6 CPUs
  • 6,700× faster
  • what took Simon 1 hr, now takes half a second.

What
is AI?

  • Let's think about this like the phone.
  • What are the core pieces?

AI is:
a neural
network.

  • In 1943, two guys named Warren and Walter build a math calculation inspiried by their understanding of the brain.
  • 15 yrs later, In 1958, a dude named Frank updated the math model so that it could "learn" by making the math model updateable.
  • A NN is a way to take an input and predict an output.
  • A NN is a computer program.
  • Let's take a simple example of what this prediciton looks like.

“The sky
is ...”

  • A NN is a math model that can predict the next word.
  • It is more than that.
  • There's a training process.
  • You give it lots of data, like all of wikipedia.
  • You have lots of humans grade it's responses and give it feedback.
  • As you loop this process, it gets better at predicting what the trainers want it to predict.

AI is:
code.

  • A math model by itself doesn't do much.
  • It's like the engine of a car.
  • But the car gives it it's true potential.
  • We sometimes call the code around the NN a "Harness"
  • When you guys go to ChatGPT .com, you're seeing the modern harness.
  • But how did we get from Frank's learning loop in 1958 to ChatGPT?
